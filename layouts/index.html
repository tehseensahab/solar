How we work

Full methodology →

  1. Primary sources first

    Utility tariffs, regulator filings and agency guidance come before commentary.

  2. Answer first

    Every article opens with the short answer, then shows the evidence behind it.

  3. Dated and verified

    Figures carry an “as of” date and a last-verified date so you know how fresh they are.

  4. Corrected in the open

    When we get something wrong, we fix it and say so on our corrections page.
