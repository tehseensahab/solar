Federal tax credits for {{ .d.name }} homeowners

Buying your own system: the 30% homeowner credit is gone. The Residential Clean Energy Credit (Section 25D) is not allowed for expenditures made after December 31, 2025. The IRS treats the expenditure as made when installation is complete, so a system finished in 2026 does not qualify even if it was paid for in 2025.

Leases, PPAs and prepaid leases: a credit can still apply, but to the owner. When a company owns the system on your roof, that company may claim the business clean electricity investment credit (Section 48E). For solar, the credit ends for systems placed in service after December 31, 2027, unless construction began before July 5, 2026. That construction-start date has passed, so a new third-party-owned system generally has to be in service by the end of 2027 to qualify. You do not receive this credit yourself; ask in writing whether, and how, it lowers your lease or PPA price.

Sources: IRS, FAQs on Public Law 119-21 (FS-2025-05, Aug. 21, 2025); IRS Notice 2025-42, beginning of construction for wind and solar facilities. Not tax advice: confirm eligibility with a tax professional. See also our solar payback guide without the federal credit.
