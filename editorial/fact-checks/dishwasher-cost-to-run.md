# Fact-check: dishwasher-cost-to-run

Checked November 14, 2026. Byline John Marshall. One independent fact-check agent for both of today's articles (with maryland-net-metering-changes-2027); findings applied. Counts: 22 verified, 12 qualified, 1 not verified (rewritten).

- EIA RECS 2020 Table CE5.3b "(kWh per household using the end use)", 7th column Dishwashers: All 112; NE 115, MW 117, S 109, W 111; members 1-6+ 66/109/128/152/157/166; refrigerators 839. Cross-checked with MMBtu table (0.38 MMBtu ~ 111 kWh). VERIFIED. RECS figures are estimates ("estimated").
- ENERGY STAR key product criteria: 240 kWh/yr standard, 155 compact, effective July 19, 2023. VERIFIED. ENERGY STAR product page: "about $50 per year to run", "less than half as much energy as washing dishes by hand", 8,400 gallons, ~$220/yr. QUALIFIED (assumptions not stated; attributed).
- DOE test procedure final rule 88 FR (Jan. 18, 2023, govinfo 2022-27879): "using 215 annual cycles"; "DOE's analysis of 2015 RECS data indicates annual use of 185 cycles." VERIFIED.
- Whirlpool WDP340PAF EnergyGuide (older label): 307 kWh, four loads a week, 12 c/kWh, $1.09/therm. QUALIFIED (one model). The $37 electric / $29 gas placement could not be confirmed verbatim (label text extracts out of order); article now states only our calculation 307 x $0.12 = ~$37.
- DOE Energy Saver (energy.gov/node/4823765) tip quotes. VERIFIED.
- Math recomputed: $20.34/yr, $1.70/mo; per load 1.12 kWh 20c, 0.72 kWh 13c, 1.48 kWh 27c; household and state tables; 307 kWh at 18.16c = ~$56. VERIFIED.
- Not verified, fixed: "2024 survey consumption data not yet out" -> "we found no newer EIA dishwasher consumption figures".
- Other fixes: description/lead "two full-size lab ratings"; "one reason" for the EIA-vs-lab gap; state table per-load column labeled ENERGY STAR max; takeaways 4-5 wording; hot-water FAQ rewritten.
