# Fact-check: illinois-net-metering-changes

Checked November 18, 2026. Byline Craig Leslie. Plan lead for Wed Nov 18 (content-plan/2026-W47.md). One independent fact-check agent for both of today's articles (with pool-pump-cost-to-run); 5 re-fetches. Counts: 17 verified, 13 qualified (wording fixed), 3 not verified (rewritten), 0 removed.

- Illinois Shines consumer FAQ (July 26, 2024): full retail NEM ends for systems interconnected after Dec. 31, 2024; "will only receive supply credits"; delivery "no longer included"; lifetime unless material modifications; ComEd/Ameren interconnection before Jan. 1, 2025; MidAmerican = completed net metering application before Jan. 1, 2025; triggers: ComEd new interconnection application, Ameren >100% nameplate increase; co-ops/municipals "may limit access". VERIFIED (re-fetched).
- 220 ILCS 5/16-107.5(n): kWh supply charges = energy, capacity, transmission, purchased energy adjustment; "gross amount of delivery services charges"; 1:1 kWh or monetary chosen at application; (d)(3) pre-2025 credits expire at end of annualized period. VERIFIED.
- ComEd 2025 changes PDF: PTO before Jan. 1, 2025; "lifetime of the system". VERIFIED.
- Ameren IL net metering page: Supply section; one-time choice; credits do not expire; $300/KW-DC; $300/KW-hour; generation rebate removes delivery crediting, supply and transmission continue. VERIFIED (re-fetched).
- Plug In Illinois (ICC): ComEd PTC 10.103c (8.413 + 1.69; PEA near zero), Ameren 10.441c / 8.262c (7.31/5.131 + 0.313 + 2.765; 0.053c unexplained gap), Oct. 1, 2026 to May 31, 2027. VERIFIED, checked again Nov. 18 before publishing.
- CUB Jan. 19, 2026 report: ComEd DFC 6.228c, IEDC 0.126c (single-family, non-electric heat); Ameren distribution 4.572c first 800 kWh non-summer, 7.811c summer. QUALIFIED (advocate summary; as of Jan. 1, 2026).
- CUB rebate sheet (Oct. 2025): lose status with generation rebate, not with storage rebate; ComEd storage rebate requires Rate BESH; Ameren residential recipients must take PTR, RTP or Power Smart Pricing, binding on future residents. VERIFIED (re-fetched). Sheet's "no less than 100 kW" line not used (looks like an error for residential).
