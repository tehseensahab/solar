# Fact-check: average-home-electricity-use-by-state-2025

Checked October 15, 2026.

- EIA-861 2025 final data page (curl): "2025 final data", released Oct 7, 2026; zip f8612025.zip downloaded; Average_Bill_2025.xlsx "Residential" tab read with openpyxl. US total: 144,800,940 customers, 874.27550 kWh, 17.23458 c/kWh, $150.67771. Bill = kWh x price verified for every row (tolerance $0.05).
- 2024 comparison: EIA sales_revenue_price table_5A.xlsx (downloaded Oct 15, 2026; page says 2024 data released Oct 7, 2025). US: 143,144,185; 863.27502; 16.479315; $142.26181. 2024 Average_Bill file not in the 2024 zip, so no cross-check of revisions (disclosed in Limitations).
- Calculations: use +1.27%, price +4.58%, bill +5.92%, customers +1.16%; x12 = 10,491.3 kWh; LA/CA use 2.48x, CA/LA price 2.50x, bills $153.32 vs $154.73 (diff $1.41); CT price 29.31 vs 17.23 = +70%; AL use 1145.43/874.28 = +31%; 21 of 51 areas above $150.68; 17 states >=1,000 kWh; Nevada -17.4% (115.07 vs 139.39), DC +23.7%.
- Definitions: EIA-861 instructions (live PDF, curl + pdftotext): customers = average of 12 close-of-month accounts; revenue includes energy/demand charges, customer service charges, surcharges, franchise fees, fuel adjustments, state/local taxes.
- Sizing illustration uses an ASSUMED 1,300 kWh/kW/yr, labeled as assumption.
- Not used: NH PUC hearing (no confirmation), Duke NC bridge rate (primary sources 403; only installer blogs).
