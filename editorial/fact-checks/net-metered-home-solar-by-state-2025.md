# Fact-check: net-metered-home-solar-by-state-2025

Checked October 15, 2026.

- EIA-861 2025 final (released Oct 7, 2026; verified on data page). Net_Metering_2025.xlsx "States- State Level" tab: residential PV capacity MW (col 3) and installations (col 8). Sum of "States- Utility Level" rows by state equals state tab for all 51 areas.
- 2024: Net_Metering_2024.xlsx (f8612024.zip, archive) has only utility-level tab; summed by state with the same method.
- US (50 states + DC): 2025 5,513,622 systems / 37,829.70 MW; 2024 5,077,543 / 34,226.26 MW; +436,079 (+8.58%), +3,603.44 MW (+10.53%). CA 2,241,381 (40.65%), CA capacity 13,786.874 (36.44%); top five (CA, AZ, FL, NY, NJ) 61.05%; CA net adds 150,398 = 34.5%; ex-CA +9.57%; CA +7.2%.
- Ratio systems/customers: customers from Average_Bill_2025.xlsx (US 144,800,940). US 3.81; HI 23.2, CA 15.6, NV 10.7, AZ 10.4.
- Growth: 20 states >=50,000 systems in 2024; OR +17.0, IL +15.8, PA +15.4, CT +15.1, WA +11.9; slowest TX +3.4, AZ +5.3, NM +5.4, HI +5.9. All 51 grew in systems; capacity fell only in SD (3.633 -> 3.572 MW).
- Definitions: EIA-861 instructions Schedule 7A (net metering definition quote; "actively enrolled ... as of December 31st"; PV capacity MW AC or DC; virtual net metering mutually exclusive; 7B separate). TPO tab separate (not added).
- IRS FS-2025-05 (Aug 21, 2025), WebFetch: "The credit will not be allowed for any expenditures made after December 31, 2025"; expenditure treated as made when original installation completed. No causal claim made.
- EIA data page (curl): next release "August 2027 (early release data)".
- Not claimed: reasons for state differences; share of homes with solar; AC/DC mix per state not analyzed.
