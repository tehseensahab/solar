# Fact-check: solar-lease-ppa-tax-credit-2026

Checked October 7, 2026.

- IRS Residential Clean Energy Credit page (reviewed Jul 4, 2026): "not available for any property placed in service after December 31, 2025." VERIFIED.
- 26 USC 25D(h) "expenditures made after December 31, 2025"; 25D(d)(1) solar water heating, (d)(2) solar electric, (d)(4) small wind; 25D(d)(2) "used as a residence by the taxpayer". Source: Cornell LII copy of US Code (not OLRC). VERIFIED/QUALIFIED (disclosed).
- IRS FAQ FS-2025-05: expenditure treated as made when original installation completed (25D(e)(8)(A)). FAQ says NOTHING about leases (checked).
- 26 USC 48E(i): denial for leased 25D(d)(1)/(d)(4) property. Solar electric (d)(2) not listed. Our reading; no IRS statement on residential leases found. QUALIFIED.
- 48E(a)(2): 6% base; 30% for <1 MW AC (among other routes). 48E(e)(4): no credit for wind/solar placed in service after Dec 31, 2027; (C) exception for energy storage.
- IRS Notice 2025-42 (read from PDF locally): sec 1 July 4, 2026 deadline; sec 3 Physical Work Test sole method except sec 6; sec 4.04 four-calendar-year continuity safe harbor; sec 6 five percent safe harbor only for <=1.5 MW AC; sec 7 effective for construction not begun before Sep 2, 2025. 2030 = 2026 + 4 (our calculation).
- Dec 31, 2027 is 14.8 months after Oct 7, 2026 -> "about 15 months" (checked in Python).
- DOE Homeowner's Guide (modified May 15, 2026 per page metadata): purchased system generally lower total cost than loan/lease/PPA.
- FTC page: 20-year contracts, payments may increase, credits go to owner, affects sale. Page returned 403 to curl; WebFetch text read; date conflicting (tool said April 2022; Oct 6 record said Dec 9, 2025). Date NOT cited; disclosed.
- NOT claimed: any company's pricing, pass-through of credit, how installers satisfied construction tests, leased battery offerings.
