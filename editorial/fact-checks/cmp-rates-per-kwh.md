- Maine Department of Energy Resources: $157.13 at 550 kWh as of July 1, 2026. VERIFIED (re-fetched; publisher name corrected from "Public Advocate"/"energy office").
- CMP $189M distribution request; PUC estimate $11.06/mo if approved (WMTW); temporary increase rejected (Maine Public, Oct. 6). QUALIFIED. "while the case continues" removed.
- NEB FAQ: kWh credits offset kWh charges; rolling 12-month bank; no payment for excess; residential not eligible for tariff-rate bill credit. VERIFIED; expiry stated as our reading.
