# Fact-check: duke-energy-florida-net-metering

Checked November 23, 2026. Byline Craig Leslie. Evergreen guide (second article), IDEAS.md item 3 (Duke Energy Florida net metering). Same independent fact-check agent as duke-energy-rate-increase-2027; re-fetched Florida Rule 25-6.065 (Cornell LII). Checker counts: 43 claims, 32 verified, 8 qualified, 3 not verified (rewritten).

- Rule 25-6.065 (Cornell LII; "New 2-11-02, Amended 4-7-08"): monthly readings; excess "credited to the customer's energy consumption for the next month's billing cycle"; "not more than twelve months"; year-end payout of unused credits at average annual COG-1 rate (verbatim confirmed by checker); payout when customer "leaves the system" (article now says leave the system, not close account); "shall continue to pay the applicable customer charge and applicable demand charge"; tiers 10 kW / 100 kW / 2 MW; Tier 1 no fees, no insurance; insurance no more than $1M / $2M; 90% of service rating. VERIFIED (same text verified for FPL Nov. 20).
- Florida PSC residential bill comparison (ba_total-2026.pdf, dated Oct. 1, 2026, Oct.-Dec. 2026), Duke column (3rd; header order FPL, former Gulf, DEF, TECO, FPU): base $94.43, fuel $41.27, ECCR $3.86, ECRC $0.40, capacity $1.33, storm $0.00, SPP $9.36, asset securitization $2.38, GRT/RAF $4.06, total $157.09 (sums); footnote $91.74 June-Nov, $102.48 Dec. 2026-Feb. 2027, weighted average = $94.43. VERIFIED. Qualified in text: base is the annualized average, not a literal monthly bill.
- DEF RS-1 SoBRA filing 01893-2026 (filed March 31, 2026; eff. June 1, 2026): customer charge $14.35; first 1,000 kWh vs additional kWh and Dec.-Feb. tiers. QUALIFIED: base June-Nov changed from $91.21 (Aug. 1 comparison, matching filing) to $91.74 (Oct. 1), so the customer charge may have changed; article says so.
- PSC 2025 Net Metering Report, DEF row (filed 04/01/2026, year ending Dec. 31, 2025): 97,362 solar + 2 wind = 97,364; 950,538 kW; 1,948,525,256 kWh delivered by utility; 675,987,636 kWh delivered by customers; $1,118,373.00 payments. VERIFIED; column direction QUALIFIED (split headers; exported < delivered is consistent).
- Duke release Nov. 3, 2026 (morningstar PR Newswire copy): "will see a $33 decrease in their January 2027 bills when compared to January 2026"; "following the Florida Public Service Commission's (FPSC) approval". VERIFIED (company). WFTV Nov. 3: additional $10 monthly reduction March-Nov. 2027. QUALIFIED (news).
- NOT VERIFIED, rewritten: "much lower than the retail price" (no DEF COG-1 figure found) -> "not the retail price" / "likely worth less"; "Duke's website did not load for us" (not tried) -> "We did not review Duke's website"; sizing advice reworded to a sourced mechanism.
- Math: 157.09/1000 = 15.7c; (157.09-14.35)/1000 = 14.27c; 400 x 0.143 = $57.20; 1,118,373/97,364 = $11.49; 950,538/97,364 = 9.76 kW; 675,987,636/97,364 = 6,943 kWh. VERIFIED.
- Not found: DEF COG-1 as-available rate; DEF net metering tariff sheet; current customer charge.
