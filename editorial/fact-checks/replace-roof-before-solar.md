Checked October 20, 2026. Byline Craig Leslie. Independent fact-check by a separate agent; findings applied.
- DOE, "Replacing Your Roof? It's a Great Time to Add Solar" (July 28, 2021): roofs 20-50 years; panels about 25-30 years; NREL estimate ~$4,000 average savings combined. VERIFIED (quotes verbatim).
- DOE, "Walk Me Through It" (Aug. 4, 2021; modified May 15, 2026): "can often save you money" quote; permit records for roof age. VERIFIED.
