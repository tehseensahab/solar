# Fact-check: community-solar-vs-rooftop-solar

Checked October 26, 2026. Byline John Marshall. Independent fact-check by a separate agent; findings applied.
- DOE Community Solar Basics (modified May 15, 2026): definition, off-site array, subscribe/own, bill credit, suits people unable to install on roofs, best-practice "No exit fees..." VERIFIED. Pronoun clash in quote fixed ("subscribers receive").
- NREL community solar page: 24 states + DC (Feb 2025); 42% households / 44% businesses can't use behind-the-meter solar (2024 study). VERIFIED.
- EnergySage community solar page: most subscribers save 5-20%; most options nothing upfront; typically cancel without penalty with 90/180-day notice; $1 credit for 90 cents example; "You'll save the most in almost every situation by installing solar panels at home." VERIFIED (re-fetched). Marked industry source (also runs rooftop marketplace).
- EnergySage H1 2026: median $2.57/W before incentives (updated Oct 7, 2026). VERIFIED.
- IRS FS-2025-05: 25D not allowed for expenditures after Dec 31, 2025; expenditure made when installation completed. VERIFIED. Takeaway reworded to "installation is completed".
- EIA FAQ: 899 kWh/month (2022). VERIFIED. EIA Electricity Monthly Update: residential 18.16 cents Aug 2026, released Oct 23, 2026. VERIFIED (preliminary).
- Minnesota Commerce: credit models, 20-25 year commitments, keep subscription when moving within territory. VERIFIED. Table wording fixed from "transferable" to "kept".
- Treasury consumer advisory questions; credit rate disclosure. VERIFIED (undated PDF).
- Atlantic City: no fees for supported projects; limited first-come capacity. VERIFIED (local only).
- Wood Mackenzie Apr 15, 2026: 10.1 GWdc end-2025 VERIFIED; outlook line was overstated (said capacity) -> rewritten as installations rebound 12% in 2026, then average 5%/yr contraction in existing programs, new programs could add upside.
- NOT VERIFIED and removed: "subscribers generally don't claim that credit; project owner handles business credits" (FAQ and table). Table availability row rewritten.
- "A few hundred dollars a year" -> "roughly $100 to $400 a year in our example". "25-year life" removed.
- Calcs: 899 x 0.1816 = 163.26; 10% = 16.33 (x12 = 195.91); 5% = 8.16; 20% = 32.65; 7,000 x 2.57 = 17,990; 17,990 / 1,440 = 12.49 years ($120/month savings is a labeled illustration). VERIFIED.
- Not state-specific: no states term; no hub updated.
