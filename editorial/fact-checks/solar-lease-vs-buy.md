# Fact-check: solar-lease-vs-buy

Checked October 18, 2026. Byline Craig Leslie. Independent fact-check by a separate agent; findings applied.
- IRS FS-2025-05 (Q1, Q7): 25D not allowed for expenditures after Dec 31, 2025; expenditure made when installation completed, even if paid earlier. VERIFIED.
- FTC Solar Power for Your Home: quotes on buying, leasing, PPA, incentives to owner, ~20-year contracts, payment increases, transfer/credit/fees, home equity loan, right to cancel; leasing company "will probably be responsible" for maintenance. VERIFIED. Page metadata date Dec 9, 2025 (visible date April 2022), so no date shown publicly.
- CFPB release Aug 7, 2024 and Issue Spotlight PDF: dealer fees 30%+; APRs exclude; $30,000/$39,000/$9,000 example (p.14); prepayment clause (p.17); UCC liens quote (p.15 fn33, CFPB citing Center for Responsible Lending); leases 15-20 yrs, no down payment, annual increases, lessor removes (p.10); PPA 6-25 yrs, escalators 1-5% (p.11). VERIFIED.
- 48E deadlines (construction by July 4, 2026 or in service by Dec 31, 2027); Notice 2025-42 Aug 15, 2025; 1.5 MW AC small solar exception: Kirkland, Sidley. QUALIFIED (law firm summaries; IRS PDF not fetchable this run). "Not past 2027" qualified for projects that didn't start construction in time.
- EnergySage H1 2026: $2.57/W median; installers on marketplace quoting TPO 14% -> 41%. QUALIFIED; EnergySage page not fetchable this run, confirmed via Mercom coverage.
- EIA 18.31 c/kWh July 2026, preliminary (data/eia_prices.yaml, Table 5.6.A released Sept 24, 2026). VERIFIED against repo data.
- Calcs recomputed: $17,990; $127.01/$38,103; $165.11/$49,534; $38.10/mo, ~$11,430; $206.57; $38,302 vs $28,800; 18.3c, 25.8c. VERIFIED.
- REMOVED/REWRITTEN: "buying usually costs less" and "cash lowest lifetime cost" (unsourced; EnergySage via Mercom reports prepaid leases/PPAs priced below cash); "company handles repairs" softened; "system adds to the sale" removed; loan term "10-25 years" removed; "workaround" characterization removed.
