# Fact-check: kw-vs-kwh

Checked November 1, 2026. Byline Craig Leslie. Independent fact-check by the same separate agent as santee-cooper-rate-increase-2027 (shared re-fetch budget); findings applied. Counts: 29 verified, 8 qualified, 1 not verified (rewritten).
- EIA Measuring electricity: "One kilowatt is equal to 1,000 Watts."; "One kWh is one kilowatt generated for one hour."; 40-watt bulb, five hours, 0.2 kWh. VERIFIED.
- EIA FAQ 97 (re-fetched): 2022, 10,791 kWh a year, "about 899 kWh per month"; updated Jan. 8, 2024. VERIFIED.
- EIA Electric Power Monthly Table 5.3: residential 18.16 cents/kWh August 2026 (17.57 August 2025), preliminary. VERIFIED (not re-fetched by checker; fetched during research).
- DOE Energy Saver (re-fetched): formula; 1,500 W kettle, 1.5 kWh/day, 547.5 kWh/yr; "The wattage listed is the maximum power drawn by the appliance."; usage monitors for 120 V devices, not 220 V appliances. VERIFIED.
- NREL ATB 2023 residential PV: 7.9 kWDC modeled system; capacity factors 12.7% (Class 10) to 19.6% (Class 1). VERIFIED.
- LBNL 2026 data update: median 7.7 kW in 2025 (DC). VERIFIED.
- Tesla Powerwall 3 datasheet: 13.5 kWh AC nominal; 11.5 kWAC max continuous. VERIFIED.
- Santee Cooper $8.00/kW current residential demand charge (May presentation; ORS letter). VERIFIED.
- Arithmetic recomputed: 899 x 0.1816 = 163.26; 1.5 x 0.1816 = 0.27; 547.5 x 0.1816 = 99.43; 0.127 x 8,760 = 1,112.5; 0.196 x 8,760 = 1,717; 7.7 kW -> 8,566 to 13,221 kWh; 10 kW -> 30.5 to 47.0 kWh/day; 13.5/1.5 = 9 h; 13.5/11.5 = 1.17 h; 5 x $8 = $40; 3 x 0.1816 = 0.545. VERIFIED.
- Fixes applied: "most common way" superlative softened; usage-monitor sentence follows DOE (220 volts); $163 labeled 2022 usage at a 2026 price; 430 W panel marked as an example and 7.7 kW labeled DC; "rated in both" rewritten; FAQ on demand charges notes some utilities average peaks; unsourced "nominal is an upper bound" replaced.
- Limitations: average price is total revenue / kWh across all utilities (includes fixed charges); examples are illustrative.
- Not state-specific; no state term or hub link required.
