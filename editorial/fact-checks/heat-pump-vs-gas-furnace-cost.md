Checked November 2, 2026. Byline John Marshall. Independent fact-check by the same separate agent as pacific-power-rate-increase-2027 (shared re-fetch budget); findings applied. Counts: 13 verified, 7 qualified, 1 not verified (rewritten).
- EIA Electric Power Monthly Table 5.3 (release Oct. 23, 2026): residential 17.23 cents/kWh 2025; 18.19 cents 2026 YTD through August (preliminary). VERIFIED.
- EIA n3010us3a (release 10/30/2026): residential natural gas $15.34/Mcf 2025. VERIFIED.

- DOE Energy Saver Furnaces and Boilers: AFUE definition; 56-70%, 80-83%, 90-98.5%; ducts excluded; electric 95-100%. VERIFIED.
- DOE appliance standards (residential furnaces): 80 AFUE non-weatherized gas (excl. mobile home), compliance Nov. 19, 2015. VERIFIED.
- DOE 2023 CAC standards FAQ (re-fetched): split-system HP 7.5 HSPF2, single-package 6.7, units manufactured on or after Jan. 1, 2023; HSPF2 = HSPF under revised test procedure. VERIFIED. FAQ gives no units: "Btu per watt-hour" now stated as common usage (was NOT VERIFIED as a sourced claim).
- DOE Energy Saver air-source heat pumps: "around 3,000 kWh (or $459)" vs electric resistance (DOE example); colder-region alternative. VERIFIED.
- Arithmetic recomputed: 1e6/3412 = 293.08 kWh -> $50.50; 100 kWh -> $17.23; 133.33 kWh -> $22.97; 15.34/1.036 = 14.81; /0.80 = 18.51; /0.95 = 15.59; break-evens 18.5, 15.6, 13.9, 11.7 cents; x50: 779, 862, 925, 1,149, 2,525. VERIFIED.
- Fixes applied: title dropped "2026" (math uses 2025 prices); HSPF2 units wording; split-system/single-package minimums; "three times as much as gas"; "more than half at rated efficiency"; DOE savings framed as DOE's example.
- Qualified (unsourced general knowledge, hedged): average price includes fixed charges; cold-weather capacity loss and resistance backup; electric-heat/tiered rates; panels produce least in winter.
