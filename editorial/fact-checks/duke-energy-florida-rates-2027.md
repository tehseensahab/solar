Checked November 24, 2026. Byline John Marshall. Rate explainer (IDEAS.md item 3, held 11-23 for lack of a PSC figure). One independent fact-check agent for both of today's drafts (5 re-fetches, 4 searches); findings applied. Checker counts: 30 claims, 19 verified, 10 qualified, 1 not verified (rewritten).

- Duke Energy Florida release, Nov. 3, 2026 (morningstar.com PR Newswire copy; re-fetched): "Florida Public Service Commission's (FPSC) approval of the company's request to lower rates in 2027"; "will see a $33 decrease in their January 2027 bills when compared to January 2026"; "Approximately $0.95 for residential customers using 1,000 kWh of energy per month" (Dec. 2026 to Jan. 2027); "an additional $10 decrease per 1,000 kWh"; "a temporary reduction DEF implements every March through November..."; C&I "Between 0.9% and 3.8%"; tax strategy avoids the 2027 base increase. VERIFIED as Duke's statement; approval QUALIFIED (no PSC order, vote or release found on Nov. 24; WFTV, Daily Energy Insider and ClickOrlando repeat Duke).
- Duke request release, Sept. 3, 2026 (finviz copy): $0.71 requested; C&I 0.8%-3.6%; "2% increase in the base rate ... is being avoided altogether"; "Some costs, like fuel, are decreasing."; "$50 million in customer savings next year". VERIFIED.
- Duke release, Nov. 4, 2025 (news.duke-energy.com): Jan.-Feb. 2026 bills "temporarily increase by $7.54". VERIFIED.
- Duke release, May 29, 2026 (news.duke-energy.com; re-fetched): Feb. 2026 "approximately $33 decrease" from "early removal of the storm cost recovery charge" (Debby, Helene, Milton); "approximately $11 decrease instituted by the company every March through November". VERIFIED. Link between the $33 Jan-to-Jan gap and the storm charge is our inference: labeled "by our reading" / "most likely" (QUALIFIED).
- Florida PSC ba_total-2026.pdf (Oct. 1, 2026; re-fetched): DEF 1,000 kWh Oct.-Dec. 2026 components (base 94.43, fuel 41.27, ECCR 3.86, ECRC 0.40, capacity 1.33, storm restoration 0.00, SPP 9.36, securitization 2.38, GRT/RAF 4.06, total 157.09); footnote base $91.74 (June-Nov.) and $102.48 (Dec. 2026-Feb. 2027); weighted average formula. VERIFIED; components sum to 157.09; 102.48-91.74 = 10.74; weighted average 94.425.
- PSC Order PSC-2026-0305-PHO-EI (Aug. 28, 2026): proposed DEF residential SPP factor 1.364 c/kWh for 2027 (= $13.64 per 1,000 kWh before taxes). VERIFIED, labeled proposal.
