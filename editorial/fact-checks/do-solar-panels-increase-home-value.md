# Fact-check: do-solar-panels-increase-home-value

Checked October 24, 2026. Byline John Marshall. Independent fact-check by a separate agent; findings applied.
- OSTI 1172644 (Selling into the Sun, LBNL-6942E): 22,822 sales, 3,951 PV, 8 states, 2002-2013, ~$4/W, ~$15,000 for 3.6 kW, host-owned only, depreciation over first 10 years, net cost as proxy. VERIFIED. $4 x 3,600 = $14,400.
- LBL news Nov 2015 (Appraising into the Sun): six states, confirmed premiums, no link to time on market, premiums likely smaller as prices fall. VERIFIED.
- LBL news Mar 14, 2017 (Leasing into the Sun): ~20,000 CA sales, 18 paired sales, no evidence of premium or discount for TPO. VERIFIED.
- Zillow 4.1% (Mar 2018-Feb 2019), NY 5.4%, SF 4.4%, LA 3.6% via Solar Power World. QUALIFIED (secondary).
- SolarInsure CA analysis via Electrek: "insurer" removed -> "a solar company". QUALIFIED.
- Fannie Mae B4-1.3-05: leased/PPA excluded; owned via sales comparison; dollar-for-dollar ban wording narrowed to equipment and installation cost. VERIFIED.
- Freddie Mac 5601.4 via homebuyer.com: exclusions, DTI, UCC-1. QUALIFIED (third-party copy). UCC-1 "usually" -> lender "may require".
- SEIA 36 states property tax exemptions. VERIFIED. FTC questions. VERIFIED.
- EnergySage $2.57/W H1 2026: EnergySage page not opened (permission timeout); confirmed via Mercom (Oct 6, 2026). QUALIFIED; "before incentives" dropped.
- Cost section logic fixed (per-watt old premium exceeds today's price; reframed around premiums tracking net cost). FAQ softened.
- Unsourced inverter-life and net-metering-transfer claims removed from buyer checklist.
