# Fact-check: new-jersey-net-metering-2026

Checked November 21, 2026. Byline Craig Leslie. Lead article for Sat Nov 21 from content-plan/2026-W47.md. One independent fact-check agent for both of today's articles (with tv-cost-to-run); 6 re-fetches + 1 search. Counts (checker rows): 14 verified, 14 qualified (wording fixed or accepted), 4 not verified (fixed or removed).

- N.J.A.C. 14:8-4.3 (Justia copy, current through 57 N.J.R. No. 12, June 16, 2025): credit "for the excess"; carry-over month to month; one opportunity to select start of annualized period; year-end payment at "avoided cost of wholesale power"; size cap = supply over a historical 12-month period. VERIFIED (re-fetched). Rule names no kWh unit for the monthly credit; "one credit per excess kWh" now attributed to PSE&G only.
- P.L. 2018, c. 17 (approved May 23, 2018): 48:3-87(e) 5.8% text quoted exactly (re-fetched); "[2.9] 5.8" in bill text. VERIFIED. "Clean Energy Act of 2018" name: QUALIFIED (common name; not in fetched text).
- BPU stakeholder notice (dated Nov. 8, 2024), Docket QO24090723: staff calculation that 5.8% was exceeded in EY2024 (ended May 31, 2024); Nov. 19, 2024 meeting; "updated mechanism of net metering for new customers". VERIFIED. Wording "opened a review on Nov. 8" changed to "issued a stakeholder notice".
- BPU tech conference notice (dated Feb. 5, 2025): Feb. 10, 2025 conference; comments due March 3, 2025; questions on existing customers and transition. VERIFIED.
- Technical conference slides: Rate Counsel, NJUA, ACE, Rockland, NJSEC/SEIA, Vote Solar, MSSIA positions. VERIFIED as proposals. Fixed: Rockland "monthly" removed; "solar groups asked for gradual transition" -> Vote Solar only; FAQ grandfathering range reworded.
- BPU order May 21, 2026 (ADI 3-year review/cost cap): "At this time, there are no new developments respecting net metering."; residential SREC-II $85 -> $77/MWh for registrations on/after July 27, 2026; EY2027 net-metered residential block 300 MW (from 250); registrations until block fills or June 1, 2027. VERIFIED (re-fetched).
- BPU order April 22, 2026 (ADI capacity reallocation): 229.8 MW of 250 MW subscribed as of March 30, 2026. VERIFIED (log).
- PSE&G net metering page: one credit per excess kWh; credits reduce the bill; contract year; market price from NJCEP, excludes delivery and system charges; peak/off-peak banks; size limited to annual needs. VERIFIED. "Anniversary True Up" term not re-confirmed today: removed from the new article (it was verified on 2026-11-16 for pseg-rate-increase-2026).
- A3974 (2026) second reprint: reported by Assembly Budget Committee June 28, 2026; does not amend the 5.8% provision but some provisions apply "notwithstanding" C.48:3-87. QUALIFIED. "Had not become law" NOT VERIFIED -> replaced with "last recorded action in the text we read was the June 28 committee report".
- Status "no successor adopted as of Nov. 21, 2026": QUALIFIED (searches by writer and checker found nothing after the May 2026 order; stated as absence of results).
- Removed: "a kWh credit offsets every per-kWh charge, supply and delivery" (unsourced) -> PSE&G-specific wording; "as California and other states have done" (unsourced) -> link to our Illinois article.
