# Fact-check: washing-machine-cost-to-run

Checked November 15, 2026. Byline John Marshall. One independent fact-check agent for both of today's articles (with solar-loans-2026); findings applied. Counts: 24 verified, 9 qualified, 1 not verified (rewritten).

- EIA RECS 2020 Table CE5.3a "(kWh per household using the end use)", 9th column Clothes washers: All 69 (MMBtu 0.23); NE 64, MW 70, S 72, W 65; members 1-6+ 46/64/77/87/98/111; dryers 680 (dryer ~9.9x washer). VERIFIED. Survey estimates.
- ENERGY STAR clothes washers page: 90% of energy is water heating; hot to warm halves energy; cold cycle less; 20% less energy, 30% less water; 14 vs 20 gallons; front-load 50% less than agitator, "about 25% more energy and water efficient" than impeller; ~300 loads/yr; ~$530 lifetime. VERIFIED ($530 QUALIFIED, assumptions not found).
- ENERGY STAR key criteria: changed April 22, 2021; IMEF >= 2.76 front-load, >= 2.06 top-load (> 2.5 cu ft). VERIFIED.
- DOE test procedure final rule 87 FR (June 1, 2022, govinfo 2022-10715): appendix J 234 cycles/yr (2015 data), J2 295; water heating counted with assumed electric water heater. VERIFIED.
- Whirlpool WFW87HED* EnergyGuide (pdf.lowes.com copy): 145 kWh, eight loads a week, 10.65 c/kWh (2007), $15 electric WH / $10 gas WH (matched by position; 145 x 0.1065 = $15.44 confirms), similar standard models 77-504 kWh. QUALIFIED (one model). Label kWh includes electric water heating.
- Math recomputed: $12.53/yr, $1.04/mo; per load 4.2c / 5.4c / 6.3c / 3.4c / 22.0c; household and state tables; 145 kWh at 18.16c = $26.33. VERIFIED.
- Not verified, fixed: label-based per-load figures had been described as machine-only; now labeled as including electric water heating; lede says 4-5 cents for the machine (EIA basis).
- Other fixes: description "homes with a washer"; FAQ 4 impeller wording; state-table note 0.349 kWh and one-decimal per-load column; criteria wording "changed on"; $530 assumptions and saving inference attributed; removed unsourced spin-speed tip and "motor, pump and controls".
