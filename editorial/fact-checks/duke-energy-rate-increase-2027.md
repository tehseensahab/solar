# Fact-check: duke-energy-rate-increase-2027

Checked November 23, 2026. Byline John Marshall. News article (lead) for Mon Nov 23; W47 plan expired Nov 22, so chosen from search (NCUC approval Nov. 16-17, 2026; parked "Duke Energy Progress NC 2027 settlement" in IDEAS). One independent fact-check agent for both of today's articles; re-fetched the Duke Nov. 17 release (finviz copy) and both SEC fact sheets. Checker counts: 48 claims, 28 verified, 18 qualified, 2 not verified (both rewritten).

- Duke Energy 8-K (Date of Report Nov. 16, 2026; tm2629986d1_8k.htm): "issued written orders (the "Orders") approving the Comprehensive Revenue Requirement Settlements"; "There are no material changes to the Comprehensive Settlements as a result of the Orders."; "The Companies expect to implement revised customer rates effective January 1, 2027."; "a return on equity of 9.8% based on a 53% equity component"; settlements "originally filed on July 17, 2026, and August 5, 2026, respectively". VERIFIED.
- DEC fact sheet Ex. 99.1: $1,002M (~15.0%) original; $286M Year 1, $210M Year 2, $496M; 3.7% average annual; ROE 10.95% -> 10.48% -> 9.8%; $10M Share the Light; data center terms (>100 MW 15-year minimum, 75% minimum billing demand, stranded generation charge); "subject to finalization with Public Staff". VERIFIED (re-fetched).
- DEP fact sheet Ex. 99.2: $729M (~15.1%) original; $185M / $153M / $338M; 10.95% ROE requested; $120M a year tax-credit return 2027-2028; $10M shareholder contribution. VERIFIED (re-fetched).
- Duke release Nov. 17, 2026 (finviz and miningstockeducation copies): "a typical residential customer using 1,000 kilowatt-hours per month"; DEP +$9.62 (Jan. 1, 2027) / +$5.89 (Jan. 1, 2028); DEC +$6.53 / +$4.66; 4.8% DEC and 4.7% DEP residential; "about 3.4%" DEP; "$20 million in new funding from Duke Energy shareholders"; data center schedule "above 50 megawatts". VERIFIED (company estimate; re-fetched).
- DEC July settlement estimate $9.39 / $5.52: from our NC page fact-check (WBTV July 14, Carolina Journal, Port City Daily citing Duke). QUALIFIED. Release does not explain the lower DEC dollars; article says so and treats DEC figures as Duke's estimate. "Changes from base rates only" removed (NOT VERIFIED: release does not say what projections include).
- NC Newsline Nov. 19: DEC 9.5% over two years. QUALIFIED (news). WBTV Nov. 18: DEC/DEP combine Jan. 1, rates stay separate (QUALIFIED; note WBTV swapped the two utilities' bill figures, not used). WUNC's "3.7% residential" not used (it is the all-class figure).
- Removed: "residential sees larger % because increase not spread evenly" (unsourced); "slightly" in compliance-filing sentence; "standard rate" wording.
- Math: 9.62+5.89=15.51; 6.53+4.66=11.19; 496+338=834; 1,002+729=1,731; 834/1,731=48.2%; 9.62x1.3=12.51; (5.9+3.6)/2=4.75. VERIFIED.
- Not read: NCUC orders and final rate sheets (not found in search); new customer charges; 2027 fuel riders.
- State term North Carolina; in-body link to /posts/north-carolina-electricity-rate-changes-2026/ (anchor "guide to North Carolina electric rates and solar"). Body note added to that page (What Happens Next; lastmod/lastVerified Nov. 23).
