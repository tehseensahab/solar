# Fact-check: hoa-solar-panel-rules

Checked November 26, 2026. Byline John Marshall. One independent fact-check agent for both of today's articles; findings applied. Counts: 18 verified, 13 qualified, 3 not verified (all rewritten).
- California Civil Code 714 (california.public.law copy, re-fetched; leginfo official page failed client error): void if "effectively prohibits or restricts"; reasonable restrictions definition; (d)(1)(B) PV "not to exceed one thousand dollars ($1,000) over the system cost as originally specified and proposed" or efficiency decrease exceeding 10%; (e)(1) architectural-modification process; (e)(2) 45-day deemed approval for associations (Sec. 4080/6528, non-public); (f) damages + up to $1,000 penalty; (g) attorney's fees. VERIFIED (third-party copy).
- Florida 163.04 (flsenate.gov 2024): "may not prohibit or have the effect of prohibiting solar collectors"; south / 45° rule; patio-railing exception; prevailing-party fees. VERIFIED.
- Texas Prop. Code 202.010 (texas.public.law copy, re-fetched): (b), (d) exceptions, (d)(5)(B) >10% production with NREL tool, (e) reasonable period, written determination ("use and enjoyment of land ... persons of ordinary sensibilities"), adjoining owners; (f) fewer than 51 units; amended 2015 S.B. 1626. VERIFIED (third-party copy).
- Arizona 33-1816 (azleg.gov): shall not prohibit; placement rules may not adversely affect cost or efficiency; fees to party who substantially prevails. VERIFIED ("against the board" wording removed).
- Colorado 38-30-168 (colorado.public.law copy, current through Fall 2025): void; 10% cost / 10% performance / 60-day limits; deemed approved if not denied or returned in 60 days; denial standards; safety; common elements; time-share exclusion. VERIFIED (third-party copy).
- Virginia 55.1-1820.1 (law.lis.virginia.gov, re-fetched): B "No association shall prohibit ... unless the recorded declaration ... establishes such a prohibition"; C "by five percent over the projected cost" / "by 10 percent below the projected energy production" (no "more than"); documentation "satisfactory to the association"; D common areas; 2023 cc. 387, 388. VERIFIED. Added note that condos fall under a separate act (QUALIFIED, not checked).
- Dollar table on hypothetical $25,000: CA $1,000, CO $2,500, VA less than $1,250. VERIFIED.
- Fixes applied: "many states" -> named five states; common-area takeaway limited to TX/CO/VA; Colorado deemed-approval wording; Virginia 5% framed as threshold, not cap; approval-step cost/output wording corrected for CA and TX; Arizona fee wording; Texas interference wording and 2015 amendment attribution; "no specific deadline" scoped to the solar sections.
- State terms: California, Florida, Texas, Arizona, Colorado, Virginia; in-body links to all six /posts/<state>-electricity-rate-changes-2026/ pages in paragraph 2. Not legal advice disclaimer in body.
