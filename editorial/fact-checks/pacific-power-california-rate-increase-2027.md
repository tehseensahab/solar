# Fact-check: pacific-power-california-rate-increase-2027

Checked November 8, 2026. Byline John Marshall. One independent fact-check agent for both of today's articles (4 re-fetches shared); findings applied. Counts after fixes: 24 verified, 15 qualified, 0 not verified (2 misquotes corrected, 1 unsourced clause removed, 1 wrong count corrected).
- Pacific Power, California Rate Changes page (re-fetched twice): "In November 2026, we submitted a general rate case to the California Public Utilities Commission." "The filing proposes a 57% increase in customer rates," "which will be partially offset by a 14% decrease in rates that will take effect January 1, 2027." "The company has requested that the new rates take effect on January 1, 2027," "although the review process could take several months." Drivers: wildfire prevention, insurance costs due to wildfire risk, inflation. CARE program; customers can submit comments to the CPUC and participate in public hearings. No $ figures, docket number, net figure or explanation of the 14% decrease. VERIFIED.
- 24/7 Wall St. (Omor Ibne Ehsan, Nov. 7, 2026, re-fetched): filed Nov. 6, 2026; $103.4 million; base revenue +$82.8 million (56.8%); residential 56.9%, overall 57.0%; "A company pricing director testified that the average residential Schedule D bill would rise $121.15 a month"; no case number yet; unclear whether figures include the 14% decrease; about 46,000 customers; Siskiyou, Modoc, Shasta, Del Norte. QUALIFIED (news report of filing).
- Seeking Alpha via TradingView (re-fetched): "typical residential electricity bill by $120/month"; "$103M/year" base revenue (conflicts with 24/7's $82.8M, disclosed in article); Flynn to WSJ on poles and insulated conductor. QUALIFIED. Benzinga: search-result headline only (fetch failed: client error).
- PacifiCorp application (pacificpower.net, docket A.26-02-___): filed Feb. 27, 2026; Slater (2020) and McKinney (2022) fires; about $340.5 million of about $637.4 million "claims costs paid and legal expenses"; proposed decision Feb. 26, 2027, final March 25, 2027. VERIFIED.
- Pacific Power California customer generation FAQ (updated Feb. 5, 2020): NEM closed due to statutory capacity; legacy NEM to March 1, 2040 for applications before March 1, 2020 interconnected by March 1, 2023; Net Billing export credits per Schedule NB-136. VERIFIED (old document).
- Fixes applied: two quotes from the company page rewritten to the page's exact wording; "only one is confirmed" -> "two are confirmed"; "separate 14% decrease" -> "14% decrease in rates" (the company does not call it separate); table label "Residential share" -> "Increase by customer class"; Seeking Alpha $120 described as "typical" and the $103M/$82.8M conflict disclosed; "settlement and legal costs" -> "claims costs paid and legal expenses"; usage sentence hedged; CPUC Public Advisor wording replaced with the page's comment wording; unsourced "Oregon and other Western states" removed; solar value phrased as avoided per-kWh charges.
- Limitations: the Nov. 6 filing and testimony were not found in CPUC search results; all $ figures are news reports of the filing. No kWh basis for $121.15. Current Schedule NB-136 export credit values not found.
