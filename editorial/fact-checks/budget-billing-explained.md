# Fact-check: budget-billing-explained

Checked October 13, 2026.

- Georgia Power budget billing page (WebFetch): current bill averaged with previous 11 months; recalculated monthly; one-month notice; after 12 months balance "divided equally over the next 12 months"; eligibility (12 months billed, good standing, not discontinued in prior 12 months); free, not a discount; enrollment temporarily paused; leaving makes difference due.
- Roseville Electric page: previous 12 months averaged; periodic review; "true-up after 11 months"; not eligible with annualized solar account; 12 months service.
- Eastern Maine Electric Cooperative page: previous year's use billed over 11 months; 10% adjustment trigger; June true-up bill; 9 months history; zero balance; 2026 window closed.
- EIA January $270 for electric-heated homes: Winter Fuels Outlook (Oct 6, 2026). EIA 4% winter rise likewise.
- Example bills INVENTED, labeled: total 1,616; 134.67; swing 117; +5% = 1,696.80; shortfall 80.80; /12 = 6.73.
- FPL, PG&E and Ameren pages could not be read; not used.
