# Fact-check: freezer-cost-to-run

Checked November 16, 2026. Byline John Marshall. One independent fact-check agent for both of today's articles (with pseg-rate-increase-2026). Counts: 9 verified, 6 qualified, 1 not verified (rewritten).

- EIA RECS 2020 Table CE5.3b "(kWh per household using the end use)", 4th column Separate freezers: All homes 570 (row: 839 617 494 570 290 ...). VERIFIED. Per household, not per freezer (wording fixed).
- EIA Electricity Monthly Update, data for August 2026, released Oct. 23, 2026: residential 18.16 c/kWh. VERIFIED (preliminary).
- State prices from data/eia_prices.yaml (EIA Table 5.6.A, Aug. 2026): LA 12.87, FL 14.99, TX 15.91, NJ 24.61, CA 29.75, MA 30.48, HI 47.18. VERIFIED.
- EnergyGuide labels: Amana AZC31T15D 14.8 cu ft chest manual 297 kWh $36 @12c; Amana AZC5216LW 16.0 chest manual 313 kWh $44 @14c, range $38-$46; White-Westinghouse CR4M81CFMM 17.5 chest manual 331 kWh @12c; Frigidaire FUF17DL 17.3 upright auto 441 kWh $62 @14c, range $58-$89; Whirlpool WZF79R20D 19.8 upright auto 525 kWh $74 @14c, range $63-$91. QUALIFIED (one model each, federal test).
- DOE (energy.gov/node/725246): chest more efficient (more insulation; cold air does not sink out); set -5F to 0F; colder "will unnecessarily increase energy use"; oversized freezers waste energy. VERIFIED.
- FoodSafety.gov: full freezer ~48 h, half full 24 h, door closed. VERIFIED.
- Math recomputed at 18.16c: 570 -> $103.51/yr, $8.63/mo, $0.28/day; 297 $53.94; 313 $56.84; 331 $60.11; 441 $80.09; 525 $95.34; 110 kWh gap $19.98; 239 kWh $43.40; 129 kWh $23.43; 65 W average; 1.21 kWh/day; state table. VERIFIED.
- Not verified, fixed: "extra $15 to $20 a year" for an upright -> "$20 or more a year in our comparison".
- Other fixes: "homes with a separate freezer" wording; 14.8 (not 15) cu ft; "backup energy" not "full freezer needs little backup power"; "little or nothing" when cycling off; payback sentence softened.
