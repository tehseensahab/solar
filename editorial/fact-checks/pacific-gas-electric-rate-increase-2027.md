# Fact-check: pacific-gas-electric-rate-increase-2027

Checked November 27, 2026. Byline John Marshall. One independent fact-check agent for both of today's articles; findings applied. Counts: 12 verified, 9 qualified, 3 not verified (all rewritten).
- CPUC Ratesetting Deliberative Meeting agenda (Nov. 16, 2026; 629923203.PDF): A.25-05-009, Agenda ID 24547, "Up to $456 million", "beginning January 1, 2027", relief to "remain in effect until implementation of a final decision". VERIFIED.
- Nov. 19, 2026 approval, unanimous: NBC Bay Area ("approved unanimously"), KMPH ("unanimously approving"), Consumer Watchdog post-vote release (re-fetched). QUALIFIED (news + advocacy; adopted decision text not found in search).
- "subject to refund": KMPH and Consumer Watchdog. QUALIFIED. True-up wording rewritten as "expected to be trued up" (supported only by the Aug. 4, 2026 proposed decision, Agenda #24426, memorandum accounts).
- About $6/month on "the average bill": NBC. QUALIFIED (no kWh/class).
- 2.2% on Jan. 1: CBS Sacramento (re-fetched), which does not mention $456M and quotes PG&E: bundled gas+electric bills "essentially flat", "within 1.5%". QUALIFIED. FAQ wording that tied 2.2% to the $456M was NOT VERIFIED and rewritten.
- "$456 million a year": NOT VERIFIED (no source says annual); removed in 3 places.
- CPUC fact sheet (Sept. 2025): $16.637B TY2027, +$1.237B (8%), 6.1% revenue increases 2028-2030, non-CARE 500 kWh electric $214.93 -> $226.11 (+$11.18, 5.2%), decision anticipated May 2027. VERIFIED. "Published when the case was filed" was wrong (filed May 2025) -> "September 2025 fact sheet".
- PG&E ERRA 2027 bill insert (A.26-05-007): $3,176M requested; ~$10.55/month at 500 kWh. VERIFIED (request). Cal Regulatory Dec. 3 preview headline lists $3.45B: disclosed.
- PG&E rate advisory Jan. 2026: "NEW Residential Bundled Non-CARE Average Rate as of January 1, 2026: 41.46 cents per kilowatt-hour." VERIFIED (re-fetched).
- Illustration 41.46 x 0.022 = 0.912c; x 500 / 100 = $4.56. VERIFIED (labeled illustration).
- Cal Regulatory: preliminary ALs Nov. 16, protests Dec. 7, final rates late Dec., non-CARE bills "would rise at PG&E". QUALIFIED (trade, paywalled).
- Overstatements softened: "three things decide" -> "three main things will shape"; payback "months, not years" -> "only modestly".
- State term California; in-body link to /posts/california-electricity-rate-changes-2026/ in paragraph 2 (anchor "guide to California electric rates and rooftop solar"). California page got a dated Nov. 27 body note linking here (lastmod set).
