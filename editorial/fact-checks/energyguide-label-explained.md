# Fact-check: energyguide-label-explained

Checked November 22, 2026. Byline John Marshall. Second article (evergreen guide, IDEAS idea 2). Same independent fact-check agent as cmp-rates-per-kwh. Counts: 13 verified, 4 qualified, 2 not verified (fixed).

- FTC consumer page: "only an estimate, based on typical use and a national average price for energy"; product list. VERIFIED.
- 16 CFR 305.12(a): label price from the appendix in effect when labels are affixed; no relabeling required. VERIFIED.
- Appendix K1: "Refrigerators, Refrigerator-Freezers, Freezers, Clothes Washers, Dishwashers, and Water Heater Labels"; 14c/kWh; $1.21/therm (87 FR 61477, Oct. 12, 2022). VERIFIED (re-fetched).
- 305.15 washers six loads/week; 305.16 dishwashers four loads/week; 305.14 refrigerator cost line. VERIFIED.
- Room/portable AC 13c (FR Oct. 12, 2022); washer ranges reflect only the tested model (dryer excluded). VERIFIED (re-fetched).
- TV: 5 h on, 19 h standby, 16c (305.25); 16c effective May 2, 2024 (FR 2024-02036); ENERGY STAR logo on TV labels only for MOU manufacturers. VERIFIED.
- EIA August 2026 U.S. residential 18.16c (released Oct. 23, 2026; September not out on Nov. 22). VERIFIED.
- Whirlpool washer labels (W11266921 PDF; Lowe's copy WFW95HED): 125 kWh; 12c and $1.09/therm, six loads a week. QUALIFIED (one model each). Model number "WFW5090G" removed from sources table (not in log).
- Fixed: "label is low by 30%/51%" framing (NOT VERIFIED: 14c label is 23% below the cost at 18.16c) -> "real cost is about 30%/51% higher than the label shows"; table header renamed.
- Qualified (accepted): label anatomy descriptions; 18.16c method note; solar paragraph (general).
- Math recomputed: 18.16/12 = 1.513, /13 = 1.397, /14 = 1.297, /16 = 1.135; 125 x 0.1816 = 22.70; 125 x 0.12 = 15.00; 22.70/2 = 11.35; 22.70/12 = 1.89.
