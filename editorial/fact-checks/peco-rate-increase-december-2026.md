Checked November 25, 2026. Byline John Marshall. One independent fact-check agent for both of today's articles; findings applied. Counts: 12 verified, 10 qualified, 0 not verified (9 wording fixes applied).
- PUC release, published 11/23/2026: "PECO | 11.759¢ | 12.965¢ | +10.3%" under "Electric Price to Compare (PTC) Adjustments for Residential Customers"; "PECO and Duquesne Light small business customers will see PTC increases of 9.1% and 2.76%, respectively"; default-service, winter-share, supplier-offers and "call utilities now" quotes. VERIFIED (re-fetched).
- PUC June 1, 2026 release: "PECO | 11.024¢ | 11.759¢ | +6.67%" (May 20 release had "PECO (estimated) | 11.024¢ | 11.572¢ | +4.97%"; the PA hub's 11.572 corrected by a dated body note). VERIFIED.
- PUC Nov. 24, 2025 release: "PECO: 10.4 to 11.024 ¢/kWh (+6%)". VERIFIED.
- PECO GSA filing April 16, 2026 (pcdocs 1923196, Supp. No. 25): "PECO's semi-annual adjustments to the GSA for Procurement Class 1 – Residential"; "a typical Rate R customer using 700 kWh per month"; "The Standard GSA 1 rate for a Residential Rate R customer equals 10.785 cents/kWh effective June 1, 2026." VERIFIED (single filing; wording says "April 2026 filing").
- PECO PTC table "Updated as of 1/15/2025": Standard Generation Charge + Transmission Charge; "includes an adjustment for Gross Receipts Tax of 5.90%"; excludes "surcharges and sales taxes applicable to PECO distribution service". VERIFIED (2025 edition; labeled as such).
- PECO tariff clean-up pages (pcdocs 1937530): Rate R and RH both "Refer to the Generation Supply Adjustment Procurement Class 1" (checker). VERIFIED.
- 12.944 c/kWh: utilitiesformyhome.com Nov. 5 ("pending"), electricityrates.com (updated Nov. 19). QUALIFIED (third party; shown only to explain the discrepancy).
- QUALIFIED inferences kept with hedged wording: next change expected June 1, 2027 (not announced); 6-month table assumes price runs through May 31, 2027; GSA share 10.785 of 11.759 (our arithmetic).
- Arithmetic: 1.206/11.759 = 10.26%; $6.03/$8.44/$12.06; x6 = $36.18/$50.65 (8.442 x 6)/$72.36; 1.941/11.024 = 17.61%, $13.59 at 700 kWh; 0.735/11.024 = 6.67%. VERIFIED.
- Fixes applied: "PECO uses 700 kWh" now cites the April 2026 filing; semi-annual paragraph rewritten; "early November / official list" softened; "among the lowest" -> "second lowest ... after West Penn Power" with West Penn row added; 2025 table labeled; distribution-exclusion wording uses PECO's text; "call utilities now" paraphrase; "most solar homes" -> "many".
