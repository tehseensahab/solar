# Fact-check: dryer-cost-to-run

Checked November 11, 2026. Byline John Marshall. One independent fact-check agent for both of today's articles (with refrigerator-cost-to-run); findings applied. Counts: 25 verified, 7 qualified, 0 not verified (4 fixes applied).
- EIA RECS 2020 Table CE5.3a (re-fetched by checker): "kWh per household using the end use"; clothes dryers All homes 680; NE 632, MW 702, S 700, W 641; 1-6+ members 391/620/809/943/1,060/1,231; clothes washers 69. VERIFIED. Household size is the widest spread in the table.
- DOE dryer test procedure (Federal Register, July 23, 2019): "assume the representative average use for clothes dryers is 283 drying cycles per year". VERIFIED. Per-load figure (680/283 = 2.4 kWh) is our estimate, labeled as such.
- Frigidaire FFQE5000QW spec sheet: 7.0 cu ft electric; 4,700 W element at 240 V; 240V/60 Hz/30 A; 5.4 kW connected load. QUALIFIED (one manufacturer model).
- DOE Energy Saver laundry (energy.gov/node/365797): moisture sensor, lint screen, cool-down, vent, air-dry quotes; "ENERGY STAR clothes dryers use 20% less energy"; heat pump "20-60 percent". VERIFIED.
- ENERGY STAR clothes dryers page: heat pump "around 70% less energy"; no vent needed; "approximately $210" lifetime (no assumptions stated). VERIFIED.
- EIA Table 5.6.A Aug. 2026 (released Oct. 23, 2026; preliminary): U.S. 18.16, ID 13.37, OH 19.38, CA 29.75, ME 32.11 cents, from data/eia_prices.yaml. VERIFIED against our copy.
- 1,375 kWh per kW (data/calculators.yaml, NREL ATB 2024); 10,791 kWh avg home 2022 (from how-many-solar-panels-do-i-need). VERIFIED (internal).
- Arithmetic recomputed by checker: $123.49/yr, $10.29/mo, 56.7 kWh/mo, $0.44/load, $0.85/hr; household and rate tables; washer $12.53; savings 136/408/476 kWh, $24.70/$74.09/$86.44; 6.3% share; 0.49 kW. VERIFIED.
- QUALIFIED (general, hedged): wash-water heating on water heater; TOU savings; dryer draw vs small rooftop system; savings "modest" for small households.
- Fixes applied: heat pump FAQ split DOE range (136-408 kWh, $25-$74) from ENERGY STAR (476 kWh, $86); "exclude taxes" -> "may not include sales taxes added to your bill" (EIA notes: revenue includes utility-paid taxes; sales taxes unclear); dryer vs rooftop output softened; intro comparison now limited to water heater with EIA figure.
