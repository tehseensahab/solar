Arkansas did not have a rate change on October 1, 2026 that we could find. Entergy Arkansas, the state's largest utility with about 735,000 customers, changed bills in steps earlier in the year and has more requests pending.

This article covers Entergy Arkansas. It does not cover SWEPCO, OG&E's Arkansas territory, or the electric cooperatives.

## Quick Summary

| Item | Timing | Residential impact at 1,000 kWh | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual rate review | January 2026 | About +$6 a month (4.54%) | Reported as approved |
| Generating Arkansas Jobs Act rider | June 2026 bills | +$5.77 a month | Filed March 2026 |
| Nuclear tax credit | June 2026 bills | −$1.55 a month | Filed March 2026 |
| General rate filing | January 2027 | About +$1.16 a month | Requested |

## The New Construction Rider

The Generating Arkansas Jobs Act of 2025 lets utilities recover the cost of new generation during construction, in yearly steps, instead of all at once when a plant opens.

Entergy's March 2026 filing covers the Ironwood and Jefferson power stations and the Arkansas Cypress solar and battery project. Talk Business & Politics reported:

- **Rider:** +$5.77 a month at 1,000 kWh, about 4.17%.
- **Federal nuclear tax credit:** −$1.55 a month, about 1.14%.
- **Net:** $5.77 − $1.55 = **+$4.22** a month, about 3%.
- **Timing:** approved changes show up in June bills. The filing repeats every year around March 1.

## The General Rate Filing

On February 27, 2026, Entergy Arkansas asked the Arkansas Public Service Commission for a base rate change effective January 2027:

- **Residential:** less than 1%, about $1.16 a month at 1,000 kWh.
- **All customers:** less than 2%.
- **New options:** optional time-of-use rates, a 50% discount on the customer charge for more than 113,000 low-income households, and a seasonal agricultural rate.

## The January 2026 Step

The Camden News reported in January 2026 that residential rates were rising about $6 a month after the commission approved a 4.54% increase. We could only read the headline, so we report it without detail.

## What This Means for Homeowners With Solar

We did not verify Arkansas's current net metering or export credit rules for this article, so we give no export rates here. Ask your utility for its current solar tariff, and check how exported energy is credited before sizing a system. Fixed monthly charges are not reduced by solar.

Riders billed per kWh are avoided by solar you use yourself. Our [solar payback guide](/posts/solar-payback-period-without-federal-tax-credit/) shows how the retail rate and the export credit change the math.

## What to Check on Your Bill

1. The customer charge.
2. Rider lines added in June 2026.
3. The energy cost recovery (fuel) line.

For help sorting the lines on your bill, see our guide to [reading your electric bill](/posts/how-to-read-your-electric-bill/).

## Limitations

Figures come from Talk Business & Politics and a newspaper headline. We did not confirm that the commission approved the June rider as filed, and we did not open the dockets. SWEPCO's separate request was not researched.
