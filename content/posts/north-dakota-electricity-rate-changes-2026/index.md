North Dakota did not have a rate change on October 1, 2026. The most recent change came about a month before, when Montana-Dakota Utilities (MDU) started charging interim rates.

This article covers Xcel Energy (about 97,000 North Dakota customers) and MDU. It does not cover Otter Tail Power or the electric cooperatives.

## Quick Summary

| Utility | Status | Change | Residential impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xcel Energy | Approved Feb. 5, 2026 | 10.37% overall, 12.92% residential | $11.94 a month in total |
| MDU, interim | Approved Aug. 26, 2026 | 12.3% | About $12.90 a month |
| MDU, full request | Pending; hearings likely 2027 | 14.5% ($34.5 million) | Not stated |

## Xcel Energy

The North Dakota Public Service Commission (PSC) approved a settlement on February 5, 2026.

- **Requested:** 19.34% overall and more than 24% for residential customers.
- **Approved:** 10.37% overall and 12.92% for residential customers.
- **Bills:** interim rates had already added $11.36 a month. The final order added $0.58, for $11.94 in total.
- **Excluded:** the cost of 23 facilities, mostly wind and solar projects in Minnesota.

**Our math:** $11.36 + $0.58 = $11.94.

## Montana-Dakota Utilities

MDU filed on June 30, 2026 for a 14.5% increase, $34.5 million a year (PSC case 26-0241).

- **Interim rates:** 12.3%, worth $26.3 million a year, approved August 26 and effective the following Tuesday.
- **Bills:** about $12.90 a month for residential customers.
- **What it applies to:** the basic service charge, the demand charge and the energy charge. It does not apply to riders.
- **Timeline:** hearings will likely not be held until 2027.

The news report we used did not say whether the interim increase is refundable if final rates come in lower. Check MDU's customer notice.

## What This Means for Homeowners With Solar

We did not verify North Dakota's current net metering or export credit rules for this article, so we give no export rates here. Ask your utility for its current solar tariff, and check how exported energy is credited before sizing a system. Fixed monthly charges are not reduced by solar.

## What to Check on Your Bill

2. The basic service charge.
3. Fuel and transmission riders.

## Limitations

Figures come from the North Dakota Monitor. We did not open PSC orders. We did not confirm whether MDU's interim rates are subject to refund.
