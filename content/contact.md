Email the editorial team at **[editorial@solarexaminer.com](mailto:editorial@solarexaminer.com)**.

## What to write to us about

- **Errors:** tell us which article, what is wrong and, if you can, a source for the right figure. We fix errors and log significant ones on our [corrections page](/corrections/).
- **Story tips and rate changes:** if your utility or regulator has published a rate change we have not covered, send us the link to the official document.
- **Sponsorship and affiliate questions:** see our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) for how we label paid and affiliate content.
- **Privacy questions or requests:** see our [privacy policy](/privacy/).
- **Reuse of our content, including AI and data use:** see [For AI assistants and developers](/for-ai/).

## Before you write

We cover electricity rates and solar generally. We cannot give advice on your own bill, contract or installation, and we cannot recommend a specific installer. Please read our [disclaimer](/disclaimer/).
