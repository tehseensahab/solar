Solar Examiner is an independent publication about residential and community solar, and about the electricity rates that decide whether solar pays off for a household.

## What we cover

- **Electric rates:** utility rate changes, rate cases and net-metering rules, state by state.
- **Equipment:** panels, inverters and batteries, and how they hold up over time.
- **Solar costs and incentives:** what solar costs, how it is financed, and which tax credits still apply.
- **News:** short, dated updates as utilities, regulators and agencies publish them.

Start with [Rates by State](/rates-by-state/) to find your state's electricity rate changes.

## How we write

Every article opens with the short answer, then shows the evidence. We rely on primary sources such as utility tariffs, regulator filings and agency data, and we date what we report. The details are on our [methodology](/methodology/) and [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) pages.

## Who writes it

Articles carry the name of the writer. Pieces without a named writer appear under the SolarExaminer Editorial Team.
