We try to get things right. When we do not, we correct the article and say so.

## How we handle errors

- **Small errors** such as typos and formatting are fixed in place.
- **Factual errors** are fixed in the article, and a dated correction note is added at the end of it saying what changed.
- **Significant errors** are also listed on this page.
- Articles that change because the facts changed, rather than because we were wrong, show an updated date and say what is new.

## Report an error

Tell us what is wrong, which article it is in, and, if you can, a source for the right figure. See the [contact page](/contact/).
