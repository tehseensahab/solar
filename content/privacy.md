_Last updated: October 17, 2026._

This policy describes how the Solar Examiner website (solarexaminer.com) handles information about you.

## What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or fill in a form. The website itself does not collect your name, address or payment details. The information described below is collected automatically by our hosting provider and by our advertising partner, Google.

## Advertising and cookies

Solar Examiner is supported by advertising served through Google AdSense.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- If you opt out, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized based on your browsing.

To learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Advertising does not affect our coverage. See our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) for how we keep reporting independent from advertisers.

## Consent for visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will see a consent message, provided through a Google-certified consent management platform, before personalized ads or related cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time through the privacy and cookie settings link shown on the site.

## U.S. state privacy rights

Depending on where you live, U.S. state laws (such as California's CCPA/CPRA) may give you the right to know what personal information is collected, to request its deletion, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. To opt out of personalized ads, use the Google Ads Settings link above, or enable the Global Privacy Control signal in your browser. For other requests, contact us using the details below.

## What is stored on your device

Besides any advertising cookies described above, if you use the light/dark theme button, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the name "theme". It stays on your device, is used only to remember your preference, and you can clear it in your browser settings. The site works without it.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies will not stop the site from working.

## Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives technical information about each request, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and may keep logs for security and operation. See Cloudflare's own privacy policy for how it handles that data.

## Children's privacy

This site is intended for adults researching home energy and solar. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Links to other sites

Our articles link to utilities, regulators, Unsplash photographers and other sites. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for them.

## If you contact us

If you email us, we use your message and email address to read and reply to you, and we keep it as long as needed for that purpose. We do not sell or share your email address.

## Changes

If we add analytics, newsletters or other features that collect data, we will update this policy and change the date at the top.

## Questions or requests

To ask a question about this policy or about your information, see the [contact page](/contact/).
