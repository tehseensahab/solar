## Federal status (checked October 2026)

| Item | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Rooftop solar and batteries you own (Section 25D, 30%) | Not allowed for expenditures made after December 31, 2025 | [IRS FAQs on Public Law 119-21](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/faqs-for-modification-of-sections-25c-25d-25e-30c-30d-45l-45w-and-179d-under-public-law-119-21-139-stat-72-july-4-2025-commonly-known-as-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-act-obbb) |
| Heat pumps and efficiency upgrades (Section 25C, up to $2,000 a year for heat pumps) | Improvements through December 31, 2025 | [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/energy-efficient-home-improvement-credit) |
| Home EV chargers (Section 30C, 30% up to $1,000 per item, eligible census tracts) | Property placed in service through June 30, 2026 | [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/alternative-fuel-vehicle-refueling-property-credit) |
| Leased or PPA solar (Section 48E, claimed by the owner) | Ends for systems placed in service after December 31, 2027, unless construction began before July 5, 2026 | [IRS Notice 2025-42](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-25-42.pdf) |

Income-qualified Home Energy Rebates are run by states. A June 2026 [factsheet](https://building-performance.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/06.2026-State-Home-Energy-Rebates-Factsheet.pdf) reports twelve states and DC had launched programs. Ask your state energy office whether yours is open.

## How this tool works

The state result reads the same data as our state guides: the net metering or export-credit status and any state incentives we have recorded, with the date the state was last checked. We do not guess. If we have not recorded an incentive, the result says so.
