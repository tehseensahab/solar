Each state page brings together what a homeowner needs in one place: the latest average residential price from the U.S. Energy Information Administration, how it changed from a year earlier, what the largest utilities have done or requested, how rooftop solar exports are credited, which federal and state incentives still apply, and our latest news for that state.

The figure on each card is EIA's statewide average: total residential revenue divided by kWh sold across every utility in the state, fixed charges included. It is a useful yardstick for comparing states, but it is not your utility's tariff, and your bill can be well above or below it. The arrow shows the change from the same month a year earlier. States are grouped by U.S. Census region.
